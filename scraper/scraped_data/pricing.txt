The details, plainly
Billing, trials, and
subscription policies.
Annual savings:
Starter annual billing saves 20% compared with monthly billing. Growth and Scale annual billing save approximately 17%.
Fees and taxes:
There are no setup or activation fees. Prices exclude applicable taxes, which are calculated based on the billing address.
Adding or removing users:
Additional users on annual subscriptions are prorated. Removing users takes effect at the next renewal.
Free trial:
The trial lasts 14 days, requires no credit card, supports up to 5 users, and includes Growth features. It does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. A reminder is sent before expiry. The workspace pauses at trial end unless a paid plan is selected. Trial data is retained for 30 days after the workspace is paused.
Payments:
Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are accepted. Billing invoices are available in the workspace Billing section. A failed payment triggers a customer notification; customers have 7 days to update their payment method before account suspension.
Cancellation and renewal:
Cancel through workspace Settings → Billing at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next renewal. There is no cancellation fee. The subscription remains active through the end of the already-paid period. Removing users is effective at the next renewal.
Refunds and billing errors:
No refunds are provided for partially used billing periods. Duplicate charges and billing errors can be reviewed by customer support. No additional refund exceptions are specified.
Discounts:
Eligible nonprofit and education organizations may receive 20% off annual Growth subscriptions. Eligibility must be confirmed by customer support.
