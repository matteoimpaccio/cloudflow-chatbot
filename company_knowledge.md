# CloudFlow — Official Company Knowledge Base

**Document version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved fictional company policies
**Purpose:** Authoritative knowledge source for the CloudFlow AI Customer Support Chatbot.

---

## 1. Company Overview

CloudFlow is a fictional SaaS company providing business management software for small and medium-sized businesses.

Its platform helps teams manage:
- Projects and tasks.
- Customer information.
- Business operations.
- Time tracking.
- Invoicing and reporting.

CloudFlow was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Portland, Oregon.

The company has a distributed team and serves small and medium-sized businesses.

## 2. Products and Services

CloudFlow offers three main product areas:

### Project Planning
- Project and task management.
- Team collaboration.
- Project progress tracking.
- Calendar integration.

### Customer Hub
- Customer records.
- Contact management.
- Customer activity tracking.
- Customer information organization.

### Business Operations
- Time tracking.
- Invoicing.
- Business reports.
- Operational management.

Some advanced features depend on the subscription plan.

## 3. Subscription Plans and Pricing

All prices are in USD and are charged per active user.

| Plan | Annual billing (per user/month) | Monthly billing (per user/month) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starter | $12 | $15 |
| Growth | $24 | $29 |
| Scale | $39 | $47 |

### Starter
- 1–10 users.
- 2 GB storage per user.
- Basic project planning.
- Customer Hub.
- Basic Business Operations.
- CSV export.
- Email support.

### Growth
- 1–50 users.
- 20 GB storage per user.
- All Starter features.
- Invoicing.
- Advanced reporting.
- Custom fields.
- Advanced integrations.
- Priority email support.

### Scale
- 10–250 users.
- 100 GB storage per user.
- All Growth features.
- Advanced security and governance.
- Priority support.

Scale is intended for organizations requiring advanced security and governance capabilities.

### Pricing Rules
- Monthly and annual billing are available.
- Annual Starter billing saves 20% compared with monthly billing.
- Annual Growth and Scale billing save approximately 17%.
- No setup or activation fees.
- Prices exclude applicable taxes.
- Taxes depend on the billing address.
- Additional users on annual subscriptions are prorated.
- Removing users takes effect at the next renewal.

## 4. Free Trial

CloudFlow offers a 14-day free trial.

Trial conditions:
- No credit card required.
- Maximum of 5 users.
- Access to Growth plan features.
- A reminder email is sent before the trial expires.
- The trial does not automatically convert into a paid subscription.
- At the end of the trial, the workspace is paused unless a paid plan is selected.
- Trial data is retained for 30 days after the workspace is paused.

## 5. Payments and Billing

Accepted payment methods:
- Visa.
- Mastercard.
- American Express.

Billing rules:
- Invoices are available through the workspace Billing section.
- Prices are displayed in USD.
- Applicable taxes are calculated based on the billing address.
- Failed payments trigger a customer notification.
- Customers have 7 days to update their payment method before account suspension.

## 6. Cancellation and Refund Policy

- Customers can cancel without a cancellation fee.
- Cancellation must be submitted at least 24 hours before the next renewal.
- The subscription remains active until the end of the already-paid billing period.
- No refunds are provided for partially used billing periods.
- Duplicate charges and billing errors can be reviewed by customer support.
- Customers can manage cancellation and renewal settings through workspace Settings → Billing.

## 7. Customer Support

Customer care hours:

- Monday to Friday.
- 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time.
- Excluding US federal public holidays.

### First Response Targets

| Plan | First response target |
|---|---|
| Starter | Within 2 business days |
| Growth | Within 1 business day |
| Scale | Within 4 business hours |

These targets refer to the first response, not guaranteed issue resolution times.

General and sales inquiries usually receive a response within one business day.

## 8. Data Export and Retention

- Customers can export project, customer, time, and invoice records as CSV.
- Data exports remain available for 30 days after subscription cancellation.
- At the end of the 30-day export period, account data is deleted from active systems.
- Encrypted backups follow a separate retention cycle of up to 35 days.
- Customers can contact support to request account deletion.

## 9. Security and Privacy

CloudFlow's fictional security policy includes:

- TLS encryption for data in transit.
- AES-256 encryption for stored data.
- Daily encrypted backups.
- Backup retention of 35 days.
- Hosting in the United States.
- Production access restricted to authorized personnel.
- Regular production-access reviews.

CloudFlow states that:
- Customer workspace data is not sold.
- Customer workspace data is not used for targeted advertising.
- Account and product usage data is used to provide, secure, support, and improve the service.

No SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification is claimed in this knowledge base.

## 10. Integrations and File Handling

CloudFlow references the following integrations:
- Google Calendar.
- Microsoft 365.
- Google Drive.
- CSV import and export.

CSV import and export are available on all plans.

Advanced integrations are available on Growth and Scale.

Files can be uploaded or linked from Google Drive. Specific upload limits and supported file types have not been established in this version of the knowledge base.

## 11. Contact and Support Channels

The website currently lists placeholder email addresses using the `.example` domain.

These addresses are not valid customer support channels and must not be presented as working contact addresses.

Until a verified contact channel is implemented, the chatbot should direct customers to the CloudFlow Contact page.

The Contact page does not currently contain a working contact form.

## 12. Nonprofit and Education Discounts

CloudFlow offers a 20% discount on annual Growth subscriptions for eligible nonprofit and education organizations.

Eligibility must be confirmed by customer support.

## 13. Chatbot Response Rules

The AI Customer Support Chatbot must follow these rules:

1. Answer using information from the approved CloudFlow knowledge base.
2. Never invent prices, features, policies, or company information.
3. If information is missing, clearly state that it is not specified.
4. Do not make promises about refunds or exceptions.
5. Distinguish first response targets from resolution times.
6. Never present placeholder email addresses as valid contact channels.
7. Do not treat illustrative dashboard data, testimonials, or sample customers as verified company facts.
8. When appropriate, direct customers to the relevant website page.
9. If a question cannot be answered confidently, recommend contacting CloudFlow support through the available website information.
10. Keep answers clear, professional, concise, and helpful.
