Security and privacy summary
How CloudFlow handles data.
CloudFlow states that workspace data is not sold or used for targeted advertising. Account and product usage data is used to provide, secure, support, and improve the service. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with AES-256. Hosting is in the United States. Daily encrypted backups are retained for 35 days. Detailed privacy terms beyond these stated practices are not specified in the approved company knowledge.
Billing and account terms
Retention and deletion.
Exports remain available for 30 days after subscription cancellation. At the end of that period, account data is deleted from active systems.
